Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the university platform.

How do students join a new course?

Once logged into the Student Dashboard, select "Join Course" and enter the 8-character enrollment code (such as ALGO2026) distributed by your instructor. Enrollment is activated instantly.

How is attendance calculated for live classes?

The platform records join and departure timestamps during live sessions. System rules automatically evaluate whether the student met the minimum duration requirement and flagged late arrivals, with teachers able to review and mark adjustments.

Can professors regenerate course enrollment codes?

Yes. From the Teacher Course Details workbench, faculty can regenerate a fresh 8-character code or temporarily deactivate codes to close course registration at any time.

Are certificates verifiable by third parties?

Every issued certificate contains a distinct reference hash (e.g. CERT-CS101-2026-ADA) that links directly to the platform's verification engine for instant validation.
